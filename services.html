our service

At GY Electromechanical Works P.L.C., we provide reliable and innovative electromechanical engineering solutions tailored to meet the needs of industrial, commercial, and manufacturing sectors. mechanical engineering

Our mechanical engineering services cover the installation, setup, and commissioning of industrial machinery and equipment. We support our clients from machinery selection and layout design through erection, training, and commissioning. We also provide air compressor line systems and plumbing works to support reliable industrial operations.

Our Mechanical Services Include:

  • Machinery selection
  • Layout design
  • Erection
  • Training
  • Commissioning
  • Air compressor line system and plumbing works

Electrical Engineering

Our electrical engineering services provide reliable solutions for electrical power, control, installation, and maintenance. Our Electrical Services Include:

  • Electrical power distribution system work

  • Electrical machine maintenance
